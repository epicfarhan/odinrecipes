Easy Egg Fried Rice

Whip up a flavorful, restaurant-style egg fried rice at home using day-old chilled rice, fresh vegetables, and a quick savory soy sauce blend.

Ingredients

The Rice & Base

  • 3 cups cooked white rice, chilled (preferably day-old)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil (divided)
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced

The Vegetables & Seasoning

  • 1 cup mixed frozen peas and carrots, thawed
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Scramble the eggs: Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat. Pour in the beaten eggs, scramble quickly until just set, then remove and set aside in a small bowl.
  2. Sauté aromatics: Add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the same pan over medium-high heat. Add the diced onion and minced garlic, sautéing for about 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add vegetables: Toss in the mixed peas and carrots, cooking for another 2 to 3 minutes until tender.
  4. Fry the rice: Turn the heat up to high. Add the chilled cooked rice, breaking up any clumps with a spatula, and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until thoroughly heated through.
  5. Season: Drizzle the soy sauce and sesame oil evenly over the rice. Season with salt and black pepper to taste, tossing continuously so everything is well coated.
  6. Combine and finish: Gently fold the scrambled eggs and sliced green onions back into the rice. Cook for 1 more minute, then remove from heat.
  7. Serve: Serve hot straight from the pan and enjoy!
