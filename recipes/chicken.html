Chicken Fry Recipe.

Making truly crispy, juicy Southern-style fried chicken at home comes down to two secrets:
a buttermilk marinade that tenderizes the meat and double-coating the chicken to create an extra-crunchy crust.

Ingredients :

  • The Chicken: 3 to 4 lbs chicken pieces (bone-in, skin-on thighs, drumsticks, and breasts work best)
  • The Marinade:
    • 2 cups buttermilk
    • 1 tbsp hot sauce (optional, for a slight kick)
    • 1 tbsp salt
    • 1 tsp black pepper
  • The Breading:
    • 2 cups all-purpose flour
    • 2 tbsp cornstarch (helps make the crust extra crispy)
    • 1 tbsp paprika
    • 1 tsp garlic powder
    • 1 tsp onion powder
    • 1 tsp salt
    • 1/2 tsp black pepper
    • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
  • The Fry
    • Canola, peanut, or vegetable oil (enough to fill your skillet or Dutch oven about 1 to 1.5 inches deep)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken for tenderness

    In a large bowl, combine the buttermilk, hot sauce, salt, and pepper. Submerge the chicken pieces in the mixture, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (overnight is even better for maximum tenderness and flavor).

  2. Mix the dry coating

    In a large, wide bowl or dish, whisk together the all-purpose flour, cornstarch, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper until fully combined.

  3. Dredge the chicken

    Remove a piece of chicken from the buttermilk, letting any excess drip off. Dredge it thoroughly in the seasoned flour mixture, pressing the flour firmly onto all surfaces so it adheres completely.

    Pro tip for extra crunch: Dip the floured chicken quickly back into the buttermilk, then roll it through the flour mixture a second time.

  4. Rest the coated chicken

    Place the breaded chicken pieces on a wire rack or baking sheet for 15 to 20 minutes before frying. This allows the coating to hydrate and stick to the chicken instead of sliding off in the hot oil.

  5. Heat the oil

    Pour oil into a deep cast-iron skillet or Dutch oven until it is 1 to 1.5 inches deep. Heat the oil over medium-high heat until it reaches 350°F (175°C). Use a deep-fry thermometer to monitor the temperature so it stays steady.

  6. Fry in batches

    Carefully add the chicken pieces to the hot oil in small batches so you don't overcrowd the pan (overcrowding lowers the oil temperature and makes the crust soggy).

    • Drumsticks and wings: Fry for about 12 to 14 minutes.
    • Thighs and breasts: Fry for about 15 to 18 minutes.

    Turn the pieces occasionally until they are deep golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).

  7. Drain and cool

    Transfer the cooked chicken from the oil using tongs or a slotted spoon and let it drain on a fresh wire rack set over a baking sheet (avoid paper towels, which trap steam and can soften the crispy crust). Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
